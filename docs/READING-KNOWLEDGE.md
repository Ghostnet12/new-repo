# The Fold reading knowledge — 2026-09-07.2

The shared, versioned reference is `server/src/tarot/knowledge.js`. The server and browser fallback both use `interpretation.js`; the guide consumes the same reference. No external AI service or ongoing subscription was added. Interpretations are composed from curated meanings and explicit rules, not generated by a human reader or a live language model.

## Coverage

- All 78 existing card meanings, both orientations, all 5 spreads and position-specific reflections. `cardStories.js` adds 156 original plain-language interpretations. The reading explains its main takeaway, three connecting parts of the spread, an everyday application, every card's position and a three-step action plan.
- Personalized connections between the actual draw and tropical Sun sign, element, modality, zodiac Major Arcana, birth card, life path and entered-name number. A complete requested natal chart supplies the calculated Sun, Moon, rising sign, houses and a planet relevant to the selected focus. Without complete birth data, calendar-date Sun sign estimates remain explicitly approximate.
- Learning guide: tarot structure, suits, courts, reversals, questions, spread interpretation; astrology planets, houses, major aspects, chart requirements and different traditions; 12 zodiac signs; numerology 1–9 and 11/22/33, calculations and related concepts.
- Name addresses the reader and optionally contributes a name number. Gender never assigns character or tarot roles. Question is quoted as context; selected focus and need change reflection prompts and actions. The engine does not infer unspoken personal facts or semantically solve arbitrary questions.
- The Birth Chart section calculates Sun, Moon, Mercury through Pluto, Ascendant/Descendant, Midheaven/Imum Coeli, 12 Whole Sign houses, apparent retrograde motion and major planet-to-planet aspects. Its interactive wheel, placement explanations, position table, house guide and aspect guide use the same calculated snapshot as the reading. See `NATAL-CALCULATIONS.md` for methods, limits and independent verification. Transits, minor bodies and lunar nodes are outside this release.

## Calculation conventions

Life path reduces month, day and year separately, preserving 11/22/33, then reduces their sum the same way. This corrects the older undifferentiated digit sum. Name values cycle A–I 1–9, J–R 1–9, S–Z 1–8; reduce total with masters. Spaces, hyphens and apostrophes are ignored; decomposable Latin accents are normalized. Unsupported letters suppress the number rather than calculating a misleading partial name. A first name is labeled a name reflection, not a full-birth-name Expression reading.

Existing birth-card convention retained: digit sum, reduce while greater than 21, index Major Arcana. Other schools use paired cards. Personal layers never weight or replace the secure random draw. Opt-out removes every personal-symbolism layer. Saved cloud readings retain the knowledge version and interpretation snapshot; merely exploring the guide does not write a profile.
